Varkh's passage opens the complete Chapter III Names Under Stone arc; after its frontier survey, an explicit briefing from Scout Yara opens the authored Chapter IV Glass and Ash expedition. Returning its Emberglass key to Yara unlocks a second explicit review and the authored Chapter V Red Gate assault through the same proven roads.
