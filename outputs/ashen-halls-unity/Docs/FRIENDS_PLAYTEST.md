# A first evening in Ash & Brimstone

This is a local Windows build with a six-chapter campaign. Extract the whole ZIP to its own folder, then run `AshAndBrimstone.exe`. Keep its Data folder and the other extracted files together. Existing saves use schema v27.

For a first session, the default four-person party is a useful starting point. Take the sewer contract, inspect the combat previews, and spend earned attribute and talent points in **Growth**. Use cover and spread out against area attacks. Returning to Midgaard for a full rest is part of the available strategy; supplies and elixirs support shorter stops on the road.

Two optional stories are available without changing the main campaign objective. Edda's favor opens around the sewer contract. After Varkh falls, Maud offers **The Honest Weight**: inspect the Chapter III Dusk Market lamp and Old Quarry Forge scale in either order, then return to her. Site services remain available while investigating. Each story offers a deliberate equipment choice; open the Pack to equip the reward.

The most useful feedback is concrete:

1. Where did you lose track of what to do next? Give the chapter and last conversation.
2. Which fight felt too easy or unfair? Give party levels, equipment, Growth spending, and whether you entered rested.
3. Did you understand what a spell, skill, purchase, or reward would do before confirming it?
4. Did saving, quitting, and continuing preserve the expected location and quest progress?
5. Was any text cut off, art hard to recognize, sound too loud, or keyboard/controller action awkward?

Include the version shown on the title screen, screen resolution, expected behavior and actual behavior. A screenshot helps with presentation problems. Avoid overwriting a useful save just to reproduce a failure.

Automated rule checks, scripted combat policies and static screen captures cover different things. They cannot establish how a new player understands the game, physical-controller comfort, subjective audio balance, or uninterrupted campaign difficulty. Those are the main goals of this playtest.
