## Optional quests and discoverability

- **Names Beneath the Water:** Edda's fourth native choice is visible from the start. Sera and Brann each have three ordinary choices and one quest choice after acceptance. Completing the sewer first is supported, but the alcove still requires an explicit search. The main contract is separately selectable from the inspection prompt. Names, disposition, delivery, and reward are independent saved stages; either contact can return a deferred keepsake. A journal lead exists before acceptance.
- **The Honest Weight:** available after Varkh, including after the ending. Maud's native root has temper, binding, the favor, and a craft submenu; both old conversation topics remain reachable. Earlier site visits do not silently inspect the two Chapter III inscriptions. The inspection dialog preserves the normal service. Settlement and equipment choices are independent, and late collection remains possible. Baseline v2.34 had no journal lead before acceptance; the candidate now names Maud as soon as the favor is available.
- **Manual map routes:** a player's marked route deliberately takes precedence over the automatic story route. The guidance marks it as a waypoint, and route actions can clear it. This is intentional; a connected playthrough should still check that a first player understands why their compass can differ from the current quest text.
- The same regional names recur at multiple depths. Honest Weight explicitly names Chapter III to distinguish its lamp and forge. Reusing those names remains a comprehension risk that static captures cannot settle.

## Verified findings and scoped response

1. **Halvard supplied the wrong post-cistern route.** His duty and road answers named the legacy Salt Cistern stair and western route, while the production continuation is east through Lanternless Cross. The candidate preserves prototype wording in its branch and gives the normal campaign the actual road. His greeting acknowledges the completed payment, and his current-duty response follows later campaign flags through homecoming.
2. **Brann repeated completed work.** His normal visit always said the roads must wait for the cistern contract; his watch answer sent a paid company back to Borin. The candidate distinguishes unfinished proof, completed payment, later road orders, and victory. Remembrance remains a visible fourth choice, and its reactions remain intact.
3. **VI's town HUD used older guidance.** After Yara's Chapter V debrief, it fell through to generic newly marked routes. After accepting VI, its stairs branch named the Red Gate assault. Both now describe the final assault and the Salt Cistern return route.
4. **The normal journal hid current work behind history.** Only the prototype branch had a general current-story row. The root agent's coordinated change puts `Current journey` first with the live HUD objective, unfinished favors next, current chapter history before older chapters, and completed favors last. Its presentation labels distinguish the journey, map, favors, and history.
5. **Maud's available favor was absent from the journal.** Its row now appears after Varkh before acceptance, explicitly marked available and naming Maud. Viewing that lead neither accepts it nor changes the campaign objective.

Correction to the early investigation: `MidgaardRatPeltsReady` also treats earned armor as ready. A valid completed save therefore did **not** make Halvard's greeting say the writ was open; it repeated his old three-chamber proof greeting. The confirmed defects were outdated route instructions and repeated completion/hand-in language.

These findings justified making the existing townspeople remember the player's work before adding a third optional quest. They change presentation and instructions; they add no unlocks, resources, rewards, or save flags.
