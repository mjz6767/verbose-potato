# Tessa's road stock

After Varkh falls, Tessa has two additional weapons for sale. Each costs 76 gold and can be bought once, independently of the other weapon and her original 32-gold fitting. Browsing and cancelling cost nothing. The final confirmation shows the price and balance, rechecks the current gold and purchase receipt, and adds the weapon to the Pack without changing equipment. Each receipt survives save/continue; a stale confirmation cannot duplicate a purchase, spend against a different game state, or confirm a later weapon quote.

| Weapon | Base values | Intrinsic | Practical choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| +3 Bridgehook Spear | 3–8 physical, speed 7, Health +1 | Holdfast: taking Guard gains one extra Guard | A warrior or paladin can use Arms basic strikes at distance one or two, including when engaged. Class techniques keep their own ranges. |
| +3 Stonecleft Axe | 5–9 physical, speed 5, Strength +1 | Sure Hew: weapon attacks gain +2 hit | Slower physical hits with an accuracy benefit; trades away the spear's reach and the Varkh sword's drain. |

The price is roughly two ordinary Chapter III victories, before story rewards. Both are alternatives to already available equipment rather than required upgrades. The spear makes a previously unreliable weapon family accessible; axes join appropriate weapon pools and use the existing Strength, heavy-feedback and ritual-object rules. No new class restriction or global combat effect is introduced.

Tessa's root keeps four visible choices: original fitting, road stock, advice, and her personal story. Her advice explains actual engagement and reach rules. Pack and loot previews identify the wearer whose range is displayed, since trained roles can retain a range floor even with a different weapon form. A staff's name does not grant Focused Casting; that bonus depends on the caster's unspent action and movement.
